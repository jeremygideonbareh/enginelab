# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Robotics, mechanical and automotive engineers (students, makers and professionals). They have a CAD assembly open in another window (SolidWorks, Fusion, Onshape) and want answers fast: what is each part, what does it measure, what should it be made of, will it survive, where can I buy it near me. They work at a desk in a bright room, usually on a laptop, sometimes showing results to a teammate.

## Product Purpose
EngineLab breaks a CAD assembly into its parts, identifies them, measures them, recommends materials, tests parts (test bench and FEA), simulates engine life and searches for better designs with a self-improving optimiser. Success means an engineer uploads a file and trusts the result enough to act on it in minutes.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, quietly beautiful. Japanese cherry-blossom (sakura) aesthetic, chosen by the owner: soft blossom pinks, sumi-ink text, a deep beni-pink accent, generous whitespace. The tool should feel like a well-made instrument: elegant at rest, exact when working.

## Anti-references
- Generic dark "engineering dashboard" with neon accents.
- SaaS cream/beige backgrounds and identical card grids.
- Kawaii or cartoon sakura: no clip-art, no glitter, no petals over data.

## Design Principles
1. The model is the hero: the 3D view gets the most space and the calmest surroundings.
2. Beauty at rest, clarity at work: decoration (petals, blossom motifs) lives only in idle and brand moments, never on top of numbers.
3. Every number shows where it came from and how sure it is.
4. One click to start: upload, demo or sample from the first screen.
5. Motion explains state (loading, progress, selection), nothing else.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for text and controls. Full keyboard use with visible focus. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Risk and stress colours are paired with numbers or labels so colour-blind users are not relying on hue alone.
